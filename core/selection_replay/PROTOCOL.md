# Fixed-policy selection replay

Designed after V9/V11 outcomes and admission ablation, before this replay's selection totals. This is a descriptive, post-outcome analysis of saved four-candidate pools, not a preregistered experiment, new generation, prospective deployment, or CodeT replication. The two 32-task cohorts and two generation profiles remain separate.

Use exactly the five previously defined predicates C9/S9/E9/A9/Pb without changing thresholds. Within each task/profile, choose the admitted candidate with the lowest original sample index (0–3). If none is admitted, abstain. Sample ordering, tie handling and admission use no expanded-suite outcome. Pb uses the recorded base-suite result, and consequently is not a cost-matched self-test policy.

Also compute the exact expectation under uniform selection among admitted candidates, rather than drawing random samples. For an admitted pool of size n containing k expanded-suite passes, the task contribution is k/n expected passes and (n-k)/n expected nonpasses. An empty pool contributes one abstention and zero passes/nonpasses. Report these rational quantities explicitly as expectations, not observed random selections.

For each of the 20 cohort/profile/predicate cells report 32 planned tasks, selections, abstentions, first-choice expanded-suite passes and nonpasses, pass yield on all 32 tasks, and conditional passage among selected tasks. Report uniform expected passes/nonpasses and yield on the same 32-task denominator. Distinguish coverage, conditional passage and total yield. Preserve a per-task audit containing the admitted call IDs, chosen call ID, frozen target result, and exact rational uniform contribution. Report paired first-choice transitions against C9 over all tasks, including abstention as a third state; retain both gains and losses. No oracle-assisted policy selection or optimum-policy headline.

Frozen P+ means passage of the pinned base and added suites, not semantic correctness. Retain unavailable target results separately if present; a missing or contradictory identity/status causes HOLD. Invalid formats remain among the four planned candidates and are handled by the declared predicates. No pooling, model ranking, population rate, significance test, new confidence interval, training effect, or causal mechanism.

Freeze protocol, analyzer and fixture bytes with both parent derivative hashes before reading natural selection totals. Verify the selector on hand-declared cases: sample 1 fails while 2 passes; a screen removes sample 1; all candidates are rejected; permutation of input rows leaves selection unchanged; numeric sample ordering; and absence of expanded-suite outcomes from the selector's inputs. Independently reconstruct the same first choices and uniform contributions through SQL directly from original native journals, with a separate predicate expression. Verify all 20 cells and per-task choices, then check parent manifests/journals/analyses unchanged.

Time-box: 30 minutes for analysis and independent verification, then manuscript integration only if the target gap is resolved. Weak signal: first-choice yield or coverage differs from C9; zero changes are retained. Kill: identity/hash/fixture/SQL disagreement or exceeding the time-box produces HOLD; no new native/model calls, cross-execution matrix, retries, or threshold search. Remaining assets: explicit-policy replay, per-task decisions, exact analytic expectations, independent reconciliation and a compact evidence table. Scientific relevance is selection behavior within the fixed pools, rather than more execution counts.
